## About Me

I build high-performance computational systems for modeling complex systems under uncertainty — from reactive-flow solvers in C++ to data-driven models for forecasting and decision-making. My work spans numerical methods, large-scale parallel computing, and machine learning, with recent focus on probabilistic forecasting, market microstructure, and automated decision systems.

Highlights include large-scale parallel solvers for reactive-flow and combustion systems, physics-informed neural operators for high-dimensional PDE systems, and an end-to-end systematic research platform for probabilistic forecasting and automated trading built on personal time.

PhD in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics (UIUC), BS in Mechanical Engineering (UT Austin).
